# Connector Ops — AI/Cyber Talent Routing Lane

Operating kit for the first connector lane, built on the doctrine in [`skills/connector.md`](../skills/connector.md).

**Lane definition (X connects to Y):** *Freshly-funded AI & cybersecurity startups connect to specialist AI/cyber recruiting firms.*

**Why this lane (evidence, not theory):**
- AI/ML + cybersecurity are the two hardest-to-fill role families of 2026 (CIO.com State of the CIO survey, Jun 2026)
- Record venture funding: $510B global VC in H1 2026 (aifundingtracker/Crunchbase); US 2026 YTD $496B across 6.13K rounds → a constant flow of companies with fresh capital + urgent hiring pressure
- Supply side is fragmented: 10+ credible AI-specialist recruiting firms, none of whom "own" the post-funding hiring moment
