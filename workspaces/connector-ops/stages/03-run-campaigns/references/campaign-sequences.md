# Campaigns — 40 Messages, Two Sides, Two-Stage Flow

Doctrine: run two campaigns simultaneously; whoever bites first becomes leverage. Demand interest is confirmed BEFORE supply is touched. Nothing below is sent until manual provisions (playbook §6) are done.

## Campaign A — DEMAND (funded startups) · 20 messages
**Goal:** discover one live, budgeted AI/cyber role. No selling, no pitching the recruiter yet.

**Sequence:** Day 0 email #1 → (no reply) → Day 3 email #2 → (no reply) → Day 8 breakup.

### Email #1 (Day 0) — signal opener
**Subject:** `the {role_family} hire after your raise`
> {FirstName} — saw {Company} raised {round_amount}. Congratulations.
> Teams your size usually hit a wall around now on {role_family} hires — the good ones are all employed and slow to move.
> I sit close to both sides of that market and route intros when the fit is real. If you're stuck on a specific seat, I'll bring the right specialist firm to you — no fee unless an intro converts.
> What's the hardest role to fill right now?

### Email #2 (Day 3) — proof of process
**Subject:** same thread
> Following up once, {FirstName}. The way this works: you tell me the seat that's open, I confirm the specialist firm for exactly that role family, and you only meet them if you like the profile. You never pay unless you accept the intro. 30 seconds to reply with the role title is all I need.

### Email #3 (Day 8) — breakup
**Subject:** same thread
> Closing the loop — if {role_family} hiring isn't a problem this quarter, no reply needed and I won't follow up again. If it becomes one, I keep a live map of specialist firms for exactly this. Good luck with the {round_amount} deploy.

### Reply handling
- **Live role + budget** → stage-2 trigger: register supply firm, send intro-within-48h. Access fee ($2,000) invoiced on intro acceptance.
- **"We have internal recruiting"** → "Understood — I'm the overflow for the seats they can't fill." Offer the 60-day-open roles only.
- **"Not now"** → log signal, park 30 days (tracker rule).

## Campaign B — SUPPLY (specialist recruiters) · 20 messages
**Goal:** register firms you can route to BEFORE demand bites. No client names yet (protect the two-stage flow).

### Email #1 (Day 0)
**Subject:** `live demand, {firm_name} practice`
> {FirstName} — I work the other side of your desk: freshly-funded AI and cyber startups with urgent {role_family} seats and no pipeline.
> I pre-qualify demand (live role, budget confirmed) before anyone is introduced, and I route one firm per intro — no client-sharing, no inbox spam.
> If you have capacity for new retained/contingent searches this quarter, I'll send you the qualified brief when the fit is exact. Worth 15 minutes?

### Email #2 (Day 3)
**Subject:** same thread
> One follow-up, {FirstName}. To route to you I need three things on file: role families you place strongest, fee structure, and current capacity. Reply with those and you're in the rotation — I only call when a brief matches your exact practice.

### Email #3 (Day 8) — breakup
**Subject:** same thread
> No problem if capacity's tight. I'll keep you on the map and route only when the brief matches your practice exactly. If that changes, reply and you're back in rotation.

### Reply handling
- **Interested** → collect: role families, fees, capacity. Mark `registered`. Their fee structure is confidential — keep in tracker notes.
- **"Who's the client?"** → "Named only after a demand-side NDA or verbal confirm — my demand partners get the same courtesy." (This protects both sides; never leak.)

## The intro email itself (Clean Intro Forge — <120 words, sent ONLY after both sides confirmed)
**Subject:** `{DemandCo} × {Firm} — {role_title}, budgeted, this quarter`
> {DemandContact}, meet {SupplyContact} of {Firm} — {one line: exactly why this firm for this role}.
> {SupplyContact}, {DemandContact} runs {Company} ({round_amount}, {city}); the open seat is {role_title}, {salary_band}, interviewing now.
> Both of you know why you're here — I'll step back. {DemandContact}: the access fee we discussed stands. {SupplyContact}: standard terms with them.
> Replying-all keeps the thread in one place.

**Rotation:** next intro of the same role family goes to the next firm on the list. Log everything in `tracker.csv`.
