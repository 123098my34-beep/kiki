# Market Map — Encoded Vertical: AI/Cyber Talent Routing

The moat is the encoded knowledge, not the tooling. This file is the encoding.

## Demand-side pressure signatures (what a live signal looks like)
| Signal | Where to detect | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| Raised Series A/B in last 90 days | funding trackers, press | HIGH — hiring surge expected |
| Role open 60+ days (ML Eng, MLOps, SOC, AppSec, Platform) | careers page / job boards | HIGH — they're failing to fill it |
| Head of Eng / CTO / CISO newly hired | LinkedIn, press | MED — new leader builds own team |
| Expanded to new city/market | press | MED |
| Job board post removed & reposted | job boards | HIGH — pipeline failing |

## Supply-side qualification (who counts as supply)
- Specialist practice in AI/ML or cybersecurity (not generalist agencies)
- Places individual contributor through director level
- Has capacity to take new clients (ask directly in supply campaign)
- Sells retained or contingent search — success-fee model compatible

## Match filters (role guardrails)
- ML/LLM/platform roles → AI-specialist firms only (Recruits Lab, Harnham AI practice)
- Security roles (SOC, AppSec, threat hunting) → firms with named cyber practice (Harnham cyber, Redfish)
- Exec-level (VP Eng, CISO) → retained-search firms only (Christian & Timbers tier) — higher fee, slower
- Never route a demand company to more than one supply firm at a time (provider rotation only on new signals)

## Match confidence score (0–5, route at ≥4)
```
+2  demand confirmed live role in AI or cyber
+1  funding within 90 days
+1  role open 60+ days
+1  supply has named practice for that exact role family
-1  demand has in-house recruiting team visible on LinkedIn
```

## Pricing (doctrine defaults, adjustable)
- Access fee: **$2,000 per qualified intro** (range $1.5–3K)
- Backend: **10% of first-year comp** on placed candidate (or 15% if exclusive)
- Qualified intro = demand side confirmed live role + budget, supply firm engaged
